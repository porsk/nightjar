Alkony: 011 settings tabs, users, account

Mockup only, waiting for owner approval. Settings split into tabs, each with its own URL: /settings/general, /settings/transcription, /settings/users, /settings/account (/settings opens General). Tabs sit at the top of the page on both sizes, so they never clash with the bottom tab bar (phone) or the sidebar (desktop). A basic user sees only General and Account. Same tokens and parts as the 009 and 010 mockups.
