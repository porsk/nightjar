Direction 4: Köd (mist)

A quiet blue-grey evening mist with a dusty rose accent. Calm and airy: entries are generous paper-like cards in a large reading serif, and the controls sit low and to the right, an extended record pill with a small pen button beside it. The recorder puts everything in one bottom sheet, within thumb reach. Source Serif 4 for text, Public Sans for controls.
