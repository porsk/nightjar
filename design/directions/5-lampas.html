Direction 5: Lámpás (lantern), blend of Alkony and Erdő

Takes from Alkony: the amber lamp glow, the big round amber record button with its halo, Fraunces and Figtree, and the warm light pooling at the bottom. Takes from Erdő: the calm ledger (hairline-ruled entries with a time column, no cards), the quiet header icons, and the control living in the thumb zone as a dock, which also holds Írás and Keresés. The deep background is a dark moss-teal halfway between indigo and moss black.
