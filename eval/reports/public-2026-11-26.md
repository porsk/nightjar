## Machine

Host: Intel Core i7-12700H, 14 cores (20 threads), 63.7 GB RAM, Windows 11, Docker Desktop (VM with 20 CPUs, 31 GB).
The eval container was limited to 4 CPUs and 4 inference threads, which is closer to a small home server.
Timing numbers will move on other hardware. RTF is decode time divided by audio length.

## Setup

- sherpa-onnx-node 2.0.3, all models int8, CPU only, language forced per clip (hu or en) for Whisper, auto for Parakeet.
- Whisper large-v3-turbo is sherpa-onnx's own prebuilt `sherpa-onnx-whisper-turbo` export.
- The Hungarian fine-tune is `sarpba/whisper-hu-large-v3-turbo-finetuned`, converted to the OpenAI checkpoint format and exported with sherpa-onnx's `export-onnx.py` (tag v2.0.3), then int8 quantized by that script.
- Silero VAD (sherpa-onnx) splits clips longer than 25 s. One clip per run needed it, nothing else used VAD.
- Data: FLEURS test split, first 30 rows by id. That is only 12 distinct Hungarian sentences (385 s) and 15 English sentences (319 s), each read by one to three speakers. Treat the numbers as a rough guide, not a benchmark.
- Parakeet "Model size" is the extracted folder. Download sizes: Parakeet 487 MB, Whisper turbo 564 MB (tar.bz2). The fine-tune has no download, the HF source is a 3.2 GB fp32 safetensors file.

## Observations

- On Hungarian, Whisper turbo (20.5% WER) clearly beats Parakeet v3 (31.3%). Parakeet's published FLEURS hu figure is 15.7%, so this small sample is harder than average, but the gap between engines is real.
- Parakeet's Hungarian errors are mostly mangled words and dropped endings ("meghatározásra" becomes "meghatárzásra", "elem" becomes "jelen"). No clip was transcribed exactly. Whisper makes fewer such errors, mostly on rare words and foreign names (Schlegel, Goethe).
- The Hungarian fine-tune is the best on Hungarian (18.2% WER, 6.4% CER), but only by a small margin over stock turbo on this sample. Its remaining errors are accent and compound-word slips ("írokból", "fémkeveréke" written as one word, "ötvezet").
- The fine-tune is worse on English (16.8% vs 4.2% for stock turbo) and in one clip drifted into repeating Hungarian words even with language set to English. It cannot serve as the only model for a bilingual journal.
- Parakeet is about 3x faster than Whisper turbo (RTF 0.12 vs 0.35) and uses about 1 GB less RAM, and its English is good (5.9% WER), but Whisper turbo is still ahead on English too (4.2%).
- Both Whisper variants are real-time-capable on 4 threads (RTF about 0.35, so a 10 minute entry takes about 3.5 minutes).

## What failed

The first fine-tune export produced a model that returned empty text for every clip (100% WER). sherpa-onnx logged a Reshape error in the decoder: the current PyTorch ONNX exporter (dynamo, default in recent torch) bakes in a fixed token count. Fixed by calling `torch.onnx.export` with `dynamo=False` from the wrapper script. The second export works.
